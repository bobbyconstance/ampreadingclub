Published Works

Why leave it up to karma when you've got two fists?

Out of jail and back in the ring, Lonely Boxer prepares himself for a title fight against the world's most popular boxer. This is where he's happy — the ring he understands. If only the rest of his life were as simple.

A dark and surreal literary novella told in fragments, with bleak landscapes punched by flashes of humour. Welcome to the third edition (re:DUX) of Lonely Boxer, a psychological deep dive into the bruised and concussed mind of a misunderstood fighter.
